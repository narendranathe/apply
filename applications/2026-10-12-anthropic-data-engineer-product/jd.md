# JD snapshot — Anthropic, Data Engineer, Product (captured 2026-10-12)

URL: https://boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5448481008
Locations: San Francisco, NYC, Seattle. Salary: $320,000 - $405,000 USD. ATS: Greenhouse.

## About the role
Data Engineer on the Data Science & Analytics team. Build the foundation that lets analytics
scale across Anthropic. Partner with Engineering, Product and other teams to turn raw data into
reliable metrics, reporting and insights; make sure teams have accurate metrics for consumer
products from idea to launch. Lead own projects that make self-serve insights possible so teams
can make data-driven decisions.

## Responsibilities
- Understand, and where possible anticipate, the data needs of partner teams; translate them
  into data models, reporting and technical requirements
- Define, build and manage key dbt pipelines that turn raw logs into canonical datasets
- Set data integrity standards and SLAs so data is delivered on time and accurately
- Build reliable dashboards that track core metrics and share insights across the company
- Build foundational data products, dashboards and tools that let self-serve analytics scale
- Partner with stakeholders to define and materialize metrics and analysis for new and evolving
  consumer products
- Shape Product teams' roadmaps from a data systems perspective
- Become an expert in their data models and data architecture

## You may be a good fit if you have
- Significant experience as a Data Engineer or in a similar Data Science & Analytics role,
  ideally partnering with Product leads to build and report on company-wide metrics
- A passion for Anthropic's mission of building helpful, honest and harmless AI
- Expertise building multi-step ETL jobs with tools like dbt, plus experience with workflow
  tools like Airflow and version control through GitHub
- Expertise in SQL and Python for turning data into accurate, clean data models
- Experience building reporting and dashboards in tools like Hex that serve multiple
  cross-functional teams
- A bias for action, and a sense of when "good enough" beats perfect
- An end-to-end mindset: take ownership of solving a problem fully, even beyond usual scope
- Comfort with ambiguity, and a habit of creating clarity and forward progress
- Experience using AI to scale your own productivity and your team's without lowering quality

## Strong candidates may also have
- Experience building a data engineering (or similar) function from the ground up in an
  early-stage or fast-growing environment

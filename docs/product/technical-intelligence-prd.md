# Technical intelligence, engagement and publishing PRD

Status: **proposed requirements; implementation and verification are tracked separately**. This document specifies extensions to the existing private Hiring & Outreach system. It does not authorize a production deployment, assert current provider grants, or certify any newly specified capability. The [acceptance and test plan](technical-intelligence-traceability.md) is the requirement-to-test ledger; the [platform capability report](platform-capabilities.md) must be consulted for dated official documentation and account-specific evidence.

## Problem and outcome

Naren and authorized agents need a shared, evidence-grounded way to research engineering work, turn it into useful technical contributions and maintain professional relationships. Their handoff must survive a change of agent or chat without inventing facts, duplicating outreach, or treating an attempted action as a confirmed outcome. The existing Notion Hiring & Outreach source remains authoritative and the GitHub Actions orchestrator remains the sole operational writer.

Personas: Naren owns facts, permissions and publishing policy; Codex, Muse and future agents research and propose within independently assigned capabilities; the central writer validates and persists state; a reviewer checks technical claims, relationship context and any manual publishing outcome. An agent starts with a bounded brief, selects one task, retrieves a dossier and only necessary evidence, then proposes an action against fresh version hashes. Another agent can resume using durable identifiers and cursors without the first chat. A technical article can support observations, hypotheses and a concrete proposal; a relevant, verified contact and post can support a human-ready engagement draft; one reviewed thesis can produce channel-specific content and, only through a permitted path, a verified publication receipt.

Success is measured separately for local behavior and live operation: acceptance tests in the ledger pass on a specified commit; each stored claim traces to evidence; retries and cross-agent handoffs have no duplicate semantic effects; brief byte counts and logical Notion calls are measured against the protocol's documented ceilings; and real hosted or manual results are claimed only with exact run and readback evidence. No metric implies that platform access, a successful hosted run, an inbox check, or publication exists until independently verified.

## Scope and boundaries

In scope: compact agent-independent state and handoff; permitted public research; evidence-grounded engineering analysis; relationship context, suppression and human-ready engagement; channel-specific drafts, audit and truthful publication states; local and hosted verification. Preserve historical records, application authorization and uncertainty holds, existing credentials and the 06:00 and 06:30 America/Chicago schedules. Routine truthful eligible applications retain V2 standing authorization and are not subject to a blanket new approval gate.

Out of scope: replacing or restarting the historical migration; duplicate Notion databases; unapproved paid services, account changes or new secrets; unauthorized scraping, private APIs, browser session or cookie transfer, bypassing access controls or paywalls; unsolicited bulk engagement; fictitious metrics or receipts; automated legal attestations, offers, assessments, personal messages or LinkedIn invitations. Public technical content is not permission to contact its author or submit a job application.

## State and evidence model

Extend the existing immutable Cloud record envelope and source database; preserve old identifiers, original timestamps and records. Version record schemas and semantic IDs for technical source/item and revision, observation, evidence-grounded signal, inferred hypothesis, opportunity, contact-topic relevance, engagement intent/outcome, content idea/revision, publisher intent/receipt, source cursor and next action. Each applicable entity or event records source identity and canonical URL, verified author or explicit unknown, first-seen and source publication time where known, observation time, content hash, freshness, confidentiality, evidence references, status and actor. Missing fields remain unknown; publication time is never invented from fetch time. Event records link to subjects without altering original evidence.

An article's canonical identity is distinct from its content revision. Conflicting author or identity evidence becomes an exception, not a silent merge. Source cursors and item revisions are durable and replay-safe. A signal cites observation IDs; an observation cites a source revision; a hypothesis is labeled inferred with confidence, alternative explanations and a falsifiable test; an opportunity links those records and specifies before/after design, constraints, failure modes, feasibility, validation, costs and trade-offs. Candidate contribution angles cite verified facts or clearly labeled independent/in-progress projects. No inferred roadmap, company policy, benchmark or personal production experience is presented as fact.

Immutable source evidence, content and private contact history remain in access-controlled storage. Briefs and logs contain compact metadata and sanitized reason codes, not credentials, original messages, answers or full copyrighted articles. Source access is task-bound, least-privileged and on demand under the existing protocol; store short excerpts only as necessary and retain links and hashes. Durable reservations and exact post-write readback are required; Notion alone is not a transactional lock. Partial snapshots, corrupted envelopes, conflicting identities and ambiguous readback block affected work or all further effects where storage state is uncertain.

## Acceptance requirements

The numbered requirements are binding and map one-to-one to named planned tests and Given/When/Then cases in the [traceability ledger](technical-intelligence-traceability.md). A plan entry is not evidence of a failing or passing test.

### Shared state and protocol — Phase 2

- **TI-01 — Durable schemas:** The central writer can persist and retrieve every state family above as immutable versioned records, retaining provenance, actor, status and exact hash without rewriting historical records.
- **TI-02 — Handoff:** An authorized agent can retrieve a compact brief, prioritized tasks, an individual contact/company/technology dossier and cursor-based changes, then a second agent can continue from durable IDs without chat history. Exact evidence requires the existing scoped grant and selected task.
- **TI-03 — Bounded context:** Normal metadata obeys the existing 8,000-byte ceiling and evidence responses the configured 32,000-byte default; logical store calls, byte counts and disclosed token estimates are measured. Exceeding a ceiling fails or requires narrower selection, never silently leaks content.
- **TI-04 — Admission and concurrency:** Two agents proposing the same semantic intent produce at most one admitted action under the common serialized writer, including restart and partial-write cases. Stale manifest/projection hashes, denied capabilities, different bytes under one idempotency key, corrupt records and ambiguous readback fail closed for reconciliation.
- **TI-05 — Compatibility and suppression:** Existing application, contact, opt-out, uncertain-delivery and historical semantics are retained in new projections; missing or stale history never becomes evidence of permission. V2 eligible application authorization remains unchanged.

### Sources and technical analysis — Phase 3

- **TI-06 — Permitted bounded collection:** Collect only allowlisted, currently authorized sources using approved RSS/Atom, official engineering pages and documented public APIs or supplied URLs. Bound request count, size, duration and retry behavior; record fetch time and access failures. Honor robots and applicable terms, rate limits, paywalls and consent; LinkedIn/X sources require actually authorized API/export or another clearly permitted source.
- **TI-07 — Identity, updates and cursors:** Canonicalize and deduplicate aliases while retaining identity-bearing parameters; changed content yields a distinct revision, repeated feed processing is idempotent, and unavailable or deleted material remains historical. A cursor advances only after safe processing and persistence.
- **TI-08 — Fetch safety:** Validate initial URLs and every redirect against allowlists and resolved network addresses; reject private, loopback, link-local, reserved and otherwise unsafe destinations and disallowed schemes, credentials, redirects and response sizes. Handle DNS changes and language/encoding without bypassing access restrictions.
- **TI-09 — Provenance and uncertainty:** Every accepted observation links a specific retrievable source revision and excerpt/citation; conflicting or unverified authors remain ambiguous; freshness is explicit. Distinguish an author's opinion from verified company policy, and classify team direction as an inference with confidence, counterexamples and falsifiable validation.
- **TI-10 — Substantive engineering analysis:** Each opportunity gives a concrete before/after design, latency, cost, reliability, security/safety and developer-productivity trade-offs, failure modes, feasibility, alternative explanation, counterargument and experiment. Validate claim/evidence coverage, novelty and depth deterministically where possible, and require human-reviewed golden examples; no fabricated benchmark or secret roadmap passes.
- **TI-11 — Personal truth:** A proposed contribution cites `docs/ground-truth.md` or separately documented independent work, preserves incomplete labels, and never attributes unrelated project work to production experience.
- **TI-12 — Cost and offline behavior:** Source and analysis operations report explicit request/token budgets and estimated cost, stop at limits and support deterministic offline fixtures without network or provider effects. Missing access yields an unavailable capability, not invented results.

### Relationships and engagement — Phase 4

- **TI-13 — Canonical contact context:** A dossier links exact verified contact identity, affiliation, topic, source revision, prior invitation/message and relevant application/referral evidence; conflicting aliases, stale role or new message after drafting block or invalidate personalization until reverified.
- **TI-14 — Outreach protection:** Preserve existing opt-outs, pending/unknown invitations, unanswered limits, fresh complete history, pacing, company caps, weekend and same-day controls. Identical engagement intents and repeated comments/likes are suppressed across agents; a failed or ambiguous action is not blindly retried.
- **TI-15 — Useful draft:** A permitted and history-safe target may receive a human-ready, concise, specific technical question or alternative tied to a source revision and verified personal evidence. Do not invent familiarity, endorsements or the relationship. An unverified outcome, like or comment does not establish progress.
- **TI-16 — Honest inbox and outcomes:** With no approved and actually granted live reader, report `inbox_checked=false`; member exports are historical and partial categories remain unknown. Separately verified human observations can be recorded with source and timestamp, without representing a full inbox check. External sends require an independently permitted path and verified evidence; absent that path the result remains a manual task.

### Editorial and publishing — Phase 5

- **TI-17 — Editorial state:** Enforce `idea → researched → technical_review → draft → revised → ready_to_publish → publishing → published_verified` with `blocked`, `rejected` and `publication_unknown` branches. Persist author, channel, thesis, audience, assumptions, source-to-claim references, technical examples, revision hash, review, provenance and meaningful transitions as immutable records.
- **TI-18 — Quality and platform fit:** Channel-specific LinkedIn, X and Substack outputs preserve a supported thesis, original wording, source attribution and minimal quotations, channel limits and accessibility metadata where applicable. Validate unsupported claims, private history, secrets, unsubstantiated performance predictions and duplicate topics; include strongest counterargument, reviewer questions and validation plan.
- **TI-19 — Permissions and manual path:** Document current official APIs, product approvals, scopes, costs, rate limits, media and receipt/metrics support separately from account-specific grants. Only a supported and granted adapter may publish under an explicit owner-approved publishing policy and quality gates; otherwise offer a copy-ready manual artifact with an honest state. Never infer authorization from a public endpoint.
- **TI-20 — One attempt and proof:** Before a permitted publish request, persist one semantic intent and exact payload/revision hashes. Changes after review invalidate readiness; timeouts, crashes, ambiguous response or failed persistence become `publication_unknown` and block blind retries. `published_verified` requires authoritative post identity plus independently verified receipt/readback, including manual evidence where applicable.
- **TI-21 — Meaningful metrics:** Record source-attributed substantive replies, qualified conversations, referral outcomes, publication failures and editorial effort with provenance and dates; raw likes alone never imply relationship progress. Unsupported metrics are unavailable, not zero.

### Integration and release — Phase 6

- **TI-22 — End-to-end handoff:** Demonstrate source → observation → hypothesis → proposal → reusable durable context; a second agent sees the first's state and duplicate suppression. A genuinely new verified message updates priority and invalidates a stale draft without assuming live inbox access.
- **TI-23 — End-to-end editorial and crash safety:** Demonstrate one sourced thesis producing three channel drafts, unavailable providers remaining manual, permitted provider attempts occurring at most once, and verified receipts controlling completion. Crashes following publication or application attempts preserve unknown states and require reconciliation.
- **TI-24 — Hosted safety and recovery:** Before hosted tests verify actual Actions capacity and billing state without purchasing or changing settings. Run exact-candidate hosted CI only when capacity exists. Keep `CLOUD_ENABLED=false` until PR #28 recovery, immutable-history coverage, complete index, encrypted restore, writer reconciliation, idempotent rerun, authorized Notion readback and existing activation gates pass. Evidence includes exact run/commit IDs and UTC/Chicago schedule mapping; local tests never stand in for hosted or Mac-off operation.
- **TI-25 — Observable release and rollback:** Report local, hosted, unattended, manual and inaccessible capabilities separately with real evidence. Preserve existing schedules, writer group and flags; a reversible rollout and rollback leaves prior suppressions and historical records intact. Record aggregate sanitized errors, run identifiers, latency, request/token budgets and costs without private content.

## Operational rollout and risk register

Dependencies are Phase 1 PRD and test plan; Phase 2 shared records/protocol; Phase 3 sources and analysis; Phase 4 relationships; Phase 5 editorial; Phase 6 integration and hosted release. Every implementation phase starts with actual failing tests, then minimal implementation, green tests, refactor, integration tests, traceability update and a reviewable PR. Each PR records exact files, commands, output and commit; proposed test names do not count as red evidence. No phase should mark downstream work complete from mocks alone.

| Risk | Required response |
| --- | --- |
| Incomplete Notion history or ambiguous write | Stop effects, preserve original uncertainty and reconcile through the central writer. |
| Unsupported provider, revoked scope or ambiguous receipt | Mark blocked/manual/unknown, preserve evidence, never fake a result or blindly retry. |
| Automated analysis hallucinates or copies source | Require cited claims, deterministic checks, human-reviewed goldens and explicitly labeled uncertainty. |
| SSRF, redirects, paywall or restricted source | Restrict and revalidate every fetch; reject unsafe or unpermitted destinations. |
| Identity or stale context results in duplicate contact | Recheck authoritative history, aliases, suppression and source revision before proposing action. |
| Cost, runner exhaustion or late schedule | Bound budgets and confirm capacity; preserve schedules and report actual execution, not desired start time. |
| New code expands production effects before recovery | Keep existing flags and writer controls; require hosted recovery gates and a reversible rollout. |
| Private information in logs, source files or model output | Minimize source content, restrict evidence access, redact logs, keep secrets out of prompts and commits. |

No owner question is needed for defaults that preserve existing behavior. Actual provider grants, legally required consent, paid services, unresolved personal facts, and an explicit policy for unattended publishing remain genuine prerequisites to their dependent effects.

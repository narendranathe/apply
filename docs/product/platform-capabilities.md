## LinkedIn evidence and limits

- [Getting Access to LinkedIn APIs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/shared/authentication/getting-access) lists Share on LinkedIn and its `w_member_social` permission as self-service. [Three-legged OAuth](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/shared/authentication/authorization-code-flow) requires the application to request scopes and the member to consent; the application must be provisioned for the requested scope.
- [Posts API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/shares/posts-api?view=li-lms-2026-06) documents content formats and permissions, including restricted `r_member_social`. It requires versioned API headers; verify a currently supported version at implementation time.
- [Increasing Access](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/increasing-access?source=recommendations&view=li-lms-2026-03) describes Community Management approval and scopes. Its development tier lists 500 calls per app and 100 per member in 24 hours, no `BATCH_GET`, and disabled social-action webhooks. Standard access is subject to review. These limits apply to that documented tier, not automatically to every LinkedIn product.
- No granted provider permissions were inspected or demonstrated for this system. Supported provider operations must remain disabled unless the precise account, product, scope, consent, policy, and effect are verified.

## X evidence, pricing, and limits

- [Create or Edit Post](https://docs.x.com/x-api/posts/create-post) documents `POST /2/tweets` for an authenticated user. [Post Lookup](https://docs.x.com/x-api/posts/lookup/introduction) documents lookup by ID, availability and edit history, and metrics. An independent lookup can support verification only when its access and returned evidence are actually available.
- [X API Rate Limits](https://docs.x.com/x-api/fundamentals/rate-limits) specifies per-endpoint limits and rate-limit response headers; respect `429` and reset times. A rate-limit retry must not blindly repeat an ambiguous write.
- As documented on 2026-10-30, [X pay-per-usage pricing](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing) lists prepaid credits, post reads at $0.005 per resource, post creation at $0.015 per request, and post creation with a URL at $0.200 per request. These are examples, not an account quote; other endpoint and media costs can apply. Prices are subject to change and the developer console is authoritative for current rates. Do not purchase credits, provide a payment method, or incur charges without the owner's spending authorization.

## Substack evidence and manual publication

[Publication RSS](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038239391-Is-there-an-RSS-feed-for-my-publication) documents the feed. [Publishing a new post](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037831771-How-do-I-publish-a-new-post-on-Substack) describes the editor, draft, audience, and scheduling workflow. It states that publication sends by email and to the app by default unless the author changes that setting. A manual task should make that consequence visible to the author before publication and preserve the chosen revision/hash and evidence of the resulting post.

## Receipt and uncertainty rules

A prepared draft, a click, a queued request, or a successful HTTP status alone is not publication proof. Store the exact channel, author, approved revision/content hash, provider post identifier and URL, timestamps, actor, and independent readback or equivalent authoritative evidence. If an attempted write times out, returns ambiguous identity, or cannot be durably recorded, mark `publication_unknown` and reconcile; do not automatically retry. Provider support does not override the single-writer, privacy, idempotency, or current cloud recovery gates.

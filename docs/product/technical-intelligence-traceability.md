## Mandatory supporting checks

- Existing unit and integration suites, workflow/schema checks and security checks must pass on the exact candidate after phase-specific tests; identify failures and environment limitations rather than suppressing them.
- Red/green evidence must include command, relevant output, commit or patch identity, and the refactor/integration rerun. A proposed name in this document is not a failing test.
- Trace every added requirement and implementation path back to its tests. Extend this ledger when a requirement is split; do not delete historical evidence when later results supersede it.
- Real hosted verification is separate: inspect current runner capacity first; preserve disabled production until recovery gates pass; link exact run, revision, authenticated readback and restore evidence. No synthetic provider receipt or offline fixture is proof of an actual send or publication.

## Phase 2 open safety work

The local schema, projections and admission path are not an operational technical-intelligence system. The synthetic `tests/test_technical_worker_intake.py` exercises authenticated proposal, central dispatch, source-admission reservation and readback, immutable result chain, a second-agent read and repeat execution. New candidate identities are actor-independent for duplicate suppression; unrelated candidate identities can enter independently. `tests/test_technical_notion_gate.py` first failed because an unreserved source was accepted; the Notion creation path now requires readback of the matching intent and alias reservations. This gate does not authenticate a caller with direct Notion write access or independently establish single-writer serialization. A proposed source is unverified: the worker requires unknown freshness, author and publication date, and storage admission is not source verification.

Reverse-linked dossiers, revision-sensitive change cursors, contact holds, type mismatches and privacy of metadata are exercised in `tests/test_technical_agent_index.py`; its new regression cases first failed before the corresponding fixes. The orchestrator test `tests/test_technical_worker_authority.py` exercises binding the trusted context to the existing prior-writer guard. These tests use synthetic stores and do not prove hosted access, real concurrency or production activation.

On the synthetic protocol fixture, the serialized brief, one-level dossier and ready-task response measured 587, 749 and 949 bytes respectively, with one logical store call each; data-only estimates were 92, 132 and 183 tokens using UTF-8 bytes divided by four, not a provider tokenizer. A fake Notion transport admission with two aliases made 57 transport calls, including five page creations; exact replay made 18 calls and no page creation. These are fixture measurements, not live Notion request rates, billing or guarantees for a larger snapshot. `tests/test_technical_notion_gate.py` verifies that admission and exact replay pass through the actual adapter with this fake transport; this integration test passed on its first execution and is not presented as a red-to-green result.

`cloud/technical_cursor.py` is an isolated cursor-checkpoint primitive: `tests/test_technical_cursor.py` first failed for the missing module, for misclassified storage failure, and for acceptance of an unrelated item; each passed after correction. It requires exact synthetic readback of admitted source and item chains and rejects mismatched source relations. It is not wired into a collector or central workflow and does not prove a source was fetched or that the feed was complete.

On 2026-10-30, additional Phase 2 offline safety tests were run red before their implementations and green afterward: `tests/test_technical_notion_gate.py` rejected an unreserved cursor and a stale exact replay; `tests/test_technical_cursor.py` rejected out-of-order and competing cursors; `tests/test_technical_partial_fence.py` detected incomplete or missing source-admission chains; `tests/test_technical_dispatch_fence.py` fenced separate intake writes and acknowledgements; and `tests/test_technical_orchestrator_fence.py` stopped live paths before any business lane or dead-letter append when a technical chain or cursor is incomplete. Cursor writes now require admitted source evidence at the Notion adapter, deterministic source-bound identity and linked sequential hashes. A malformed predecessor stops the full source snapshot. Intake, worker and live-lane preflight use complete-source scans. These scans add provider requests and latency; actual production cost and capacity are unmeasured. No test here proves real Notion authorization, true exclusivity, a complete feed, or recovery from a write invisible to all reads. The source-only cursor fixture proves a checkpoint boundary, not completeness of a real feed batch; collector integration must require independent batch completeness and preserve the prior-writer fence.

The local full suite on the pinned bundled Python ran 779 tests successfully with one LaTeX-dependent skip; the separate legacy suite passed 85 tests. Schedule/single-writer workflow validation and compile checks passed locally. These checks do not satisfy hosted CI or production cutover.

The continuation adds fail-closed guards, each backed by an observed failing test before the change: `tests/test_technical_main_fence.py` prevents a live entry-point dead-letter before the technical storage preflight; `tests/test_technical_intake_guard.py` rejects technical intake without host-supplied previous-writer reconciliation; `tests/test_technical_evidence_gate.py` keeps technical actions blocked until independent evidence verification exists; and `tests/test_technical_cursor_closed.py` rejects new Notion cursor writes until a reviewed complete feed-cohort verifier exists. A known prewrite cursor refusal is reported as unavailable, not an ambiguous write. These are local-only safeguards and do not prove that a host assertion is genuine, repair previous workflows, or establish cross-workflow recovery. Source parsing and analysis may be developed offline while these gates remain closed, but later phases cannot be marked integrated or production-ready on that basis.

Further local Phase 2 work adds actor-independent alias reservations for technical intake in `cloud/technical_intake_aliases.py`, checked by dispatch, worker and the full-ledger preflight. `tests/test_technical_semantic_alias.py` exercises conflicting agents, immutable revisions, read-only replays and interrupted chains with synthetic stores; the initial missing-reservation cases were observed failing before implementation. This is not proof that a write invisible to subsequent reads has been reconciled. The URL identity tests in `tests/test_technical_records.py` were observed failing on repeated query-value reordering, control characters, port zero and trailing path separators before their respective corrections. Exact canonicalization changes require compatibility review before any live technical records are admitted.

Independent review added two additional red/green safeguards. `tests/test_intake_snapshot_readback.py` checks that a missing or conflicting exact read of the intake, queue, acknowledgement or semantic reservation stops before any append when it disagrees with the complete snapshot. `tests/test_technical_cross_chain.py` checks that intake bindings and already admitted source aliases cannot have different owners. Both use synthetic snapshots and cannot establish that an attempted write absent from all reads is safe to retry.

## Phase 3 fixture-only work

`cloud/technical_feeds.py` and `tests/test_technical_feeds.py` cover bounded RSS/Atom parsing, malformed input and conflicting author fixtures; `cloud/technical_analysis.py` and `tests/test_technical_analysis.py` cover structurally cited observations and explicitly uncertain hypotheses. Relevant missing-behavior tests were run red and green locally. Feed-entry hashes describe normalized fixture fields, not independently fetched article bytes, and the analysis reports that source verification, semantic support and novelty are not established. These modules perform no network fetch or publication, and they do not satisfy TI-06 through TI-12: source permission, safe transport and redirects, complete feed cohorts, verified citations, human-reviewed quality, fact provenance and real cost controls remain pending.

Before Phase 2 can exit, review the actual sole-writer wiring and access boundaries; verify fresh authoritative write validation and recovery across real restarts, complete feed-cohort accounting before durable cursor advancement, evidence freshness/hash checking, live Notion request cost, and exact hosted integration. The primitive trusts the host's prior-writer reconciliation assertion. A full-snapshot alias conflict check is not a lock, and an absent row after an ambiguous request cannot prove safety to retry. Receipt and technical-analysis evidence need kind-specific verification before they can establish an outcome. Legacy-compatible evidence and relationship identifiers are not guaranteed free of private information simply because their syntax is bounded. Do not deploy or claim an unattended worker from these local tests.

## Mandatory supporting checks

- Existing unit and integration suites, workflow/schema checks and security checks must pass on the exact candidate after phase-specific tests; identify failures and environment limitations rather than suppressing them.
- Red/green evidence must include command, relevant output, commit or patch identity, and the refactor/integration rerun. A proposed name in this document is not a failing test.
- Trace every added requirement and implementation path back to its tests. Extend this ledger when a requirement is split; do not delete historical evidence when later results supersede it.
- Real hosted verification is separate: inspect current runner capacity first; preserve disabled production until recovery gates pass; link exact run, revision, authenticated readback and restore evidence. No synthetic provider receipt or offline fixture is proof of an actual send or publication.

## Phase 2 open safety work

The local schema, projections and isolated admission primitive are not an operational technical-intelligence system. Before Phase 2 can exit, integrate and verify centrally serialized technical alias reservations and fresh authoritative write validation across restarts; durable technical intake and verified post-write readback; cross-agent technical task admission and safe execution; cursor advancement only after persistence; reverse-linked dossiers and evidence freshness/hash checks. The primitive trusts the host's prior-writer reconciliation assertion and is not authenticated intake. A full-snapshot alias conflict check is not a lock, and an absent row after an ambiguous request cannot prove safety to retry. Receipt and technical-analysis evidence need kind-specific verification before they can establish an outcome. Legacy-compatible evidence and relationship identifiers are not guaranteed free of private information simply because their syntax is bounded. Do not deploy or claim an unattended worker from these local tests.
